## What is Organic Yarn?

**Organic yarn** is produced from fibers cultivated using organic farming practices that avoid synthetic pesticides, chemical fertilizers, and genetically modified seeds under applicable organic standards. The result is yarn suitable for businesses interested in sustainable textile production and environmentally conscious sourcing.

## Why Organic Yarn is Growing in Demand

The global textile industry is increasingly adopting **sustainable yarn** for premium clothing, babywear, knitwear, and home textiles.
